# Payment FAQ - VoiceOps Store

## Accepted Payment Methods
- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover credit and debit cards
- PayPal
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- Store credit / Gift cards
- Afterpay (Buy Now, Pay Later) for orders between $35 and $1,000

## Payment Security
- All transactions are encrypted using SSL/TLS.
- We do not store your full credit card information on our servers.
- We comply with PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard).
- Suspicious transactions may be flagged for additional verification.

## Common Payment Issues

### Card Declined
- Verify your card number, expiration date, and CVV are correct.
- Ensure you have sufficient funds or credit available.
- Check with your bank if they are blocking online transactions.
- Try using a different payment method.
- Contact your bank to authorize the transaction.

### Double Charges
- Sometimes a pending charge and a final charge may appear simultaneously.
- Pending charges typically drop off within 3-5 business days.
- If both charges post, contact our support team with your order number.

### Payment Not Processed
- Check your email for order confirmation.
- If no confirmation is received, the payment may not have been processed.
- Try placing the order again.
- If you see a charge but no confirmation, contact support immediately.

### Refund Not Received
- Refunds take 5-7 business days to process after approval.
- Credit card refunds may take an additional 3-5 business days to appear.
- Check with your bank if the timeframe has passed.
- Contact support with your return tracking number.

## Gift Cards
- Gift cards can be purchased in denominations of $25, $50, $100, and $200.
- Gift cards do not expire.
- Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash.
- Lost or stolen gift cards cannot be replaced unless proof of purchase is provided.

## Billing Address
- Your billing address must match the address on file with your payment provider.
- If your billing address has changed, update it with your bank before placing an order.
- Mismatched billing addresses may result in payment failure.

## Currency
- All prices are listed in US Dollars (USD).
- International customers may be charged conversion fees by their bank.
- The exchange rate applied is determined by your financial institution.
