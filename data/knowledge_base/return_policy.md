# Return Policy - VoiceOps Store

## Return Window
- Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- The return window begins on the date the item is delivered, not the date of purchase.
- Items returned after the 30-day window will not be accepted.

## Eligibility
- Items must be in their original condition, unworn, unwashed, and with all tags attached.
- Items must be in their original packaging.
- The following items are NOT eligible for return:
  - Personalized or customized items
  - Clearance/final sale items
  - Gift cards
  - Intimate apparel
  - Swimwear with hygiene liner removed
  - Items marked as non-returnable on the product page

## Return Process
1. Log into your account and navigate to your order history.
2. Select the order containing the item you wish to return.
3. Click "Request Return" and select the reason for return.
4. Print the prepaid return shipping label.
5. Pack the item securely in its original packaging.
6. Drop off the package at any authorized shipping location.

## Return Shipping
- Return shipping is free for defective or incorrectly shipped items.
- For other returns, a $7.99 return shipping fee will be deducted from your refund.
- We provide a prepaid return label for all eligible returns.

## Refund Processing
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
- You will receive an email confirmation once your refund has been processed.
- Please allow an additional 3-5 business days for the refund to appear on your statement.

## Exchanges
- We do not currently offer direct exchanges.
- To exchange an item, please return the original item and place a new order.
- We recommend placing your new order immediately to avoid the item going out of stock.

## Damaged or Defective Items
- If you received a damaged or defective item, please contact support within 48 hours.
- We will provide a free return label and expedite your replacement or refund.
- Please include photos of the damage when contacting support.
