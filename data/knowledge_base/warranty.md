# Warranty Information - VoiceOps Store

## Standard Warranty
- All products sold by VoiceOps Store come with a 1-year limited warranty.
- The warranty covers manufacturing defects and material defects.
- The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear.
- The warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, accidents, or unauthorized modifications.

## Extended Warranty
- Extended warranty plans are available for select products at checkout.
- Extended warranty adds an additional 1 or 2 years of coverage.
- Extended warranty must be purchased at the time of the original order.
- Extended warranty cannot be added after purchase.

## What's Covered
- Defective materials or workmanship
- Component failure under normal use
- Software issues (for electronic products) within the first 90 days
- Battery defects (for applicable products) within the first 6 months

## What's Not Covered
- Physical damage (drops, spills, impacts)
- Water damage unless the product is rated as water-resistant
- Cosmetic damage (scratches, dents that don't affect functionality)
- Damage from unauthorized repair or modification
- Normal battery degradation over time
- Accessories and consumable parts

## Making a Warranty Claim
1. Contact customer support with your order number and a description of the issue.
2. Provide photos or videos demonstrating the defect.
3. Our team will review your claim within 3-5 business days.
4. If approved, you will receive a prepaid return label.
5. After inspection, we will repair, replace, or refund the product.

## Warranty Resolution
- We will first attempt to repair the product.
- If repair is not possible, a replacement of the same or equivalent model will be provided.
- If the product is discontinued and no equivalent is available, a full refund will be issued.
- Resolution is at the sole discretion of VoiceOps Store.

## Warranty Transferability
- The warranty is non-transferable and applies only to the original purchaser.
- Proof of purchase is required for all warranty claims.
