# Cancellation Policy - VoiceOps Store

## Order Cancellation Window
- Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placement if they have not entered processing.
- Once an order has been confirmed and is being processed, it may not be eligible for cancellation.
- Orders that have shipped cannot be cancelled and must follow the return process.

## Cancellation Eligibility
- Orders with status "pending" or "confirmed" can be cancelled.
- Orders with status "processing", "shipped", or "delivered" cannot be cancelled directly.
- Custom or personalized orders cannot be cancelled once production has begun.
- Bundle deals: all items in the bundle must be cancelled together.

## How to Cancel
1. Log into your account.
2. Go to your order history.
3. Select the order you wish to cancel.
4. Click "Cancel Order" if the option is available.
5. Confirm the cancellation.

## Refund for Cancelled Orders
- Full refund is issued for orders cancelled before processing.
- Refund is processed within 1-2 business days of cancellation.
- The refund will appear on your original payment method within 3-5 business days.

## Partial Cancellations
- Individual items within an order may be cancelled if the order has not been shipped.
- The refund will reflect only the cancelled items and applicable shipping adjustments.
- If the remaining order total falls below the free shipping threshold, original shipping charges may apply.

## Automatic Cancellations
- Orders may be automatically cancelled if payment fails verification.
- Orders may be automatically cancelled if the shipping address cannot be verified.
- We will notify you via email if your order is automatically cancelled.
