# Shipping Policy - VoiceOps Store

## Standard Shipping
- Standard shipping is available for all domestic orders.
- Delivery takes 5-7 business days from the date of shipment.
- Standard shipping is free for orders over $50.
- Orders under $50 are charged a flat rate of $5.99 for standard shipping.

## Express Shipping
- Express shipping delivers within 2-3 business days.
- Express shipping costs $12.99 for all orders.
- Express shipping is available for orders placed before 2:00 PM EST on business days.

## Overnight Shipping
- Overnight shipping delivers the next business day.
- Overnight shipping costs $24.99.
- Orders must be placed before 12:00 PM EST for next-day delivery.
- Overnight shipping is not available for PO Boxes.

## International Shipping
- International shipping is available to select countries.
- Delivery takes 10-21 business days depending on the destination.
- International shipping costs vary by destination and package weight.
- Customs duties and taxes are the responsibility of the customer.

## Shipping Restrictions
- We do not ship to APO/FPO addresses.
- Hazardous materials have specific shipping restrictions.
- Some items may not be eligible for express or overnight shipping due to size or weight.

## Tracking
- All orders include tracking information.
- Tracking numbers are sent via email once the order has shipped.
- Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to update after shipment.
- You can track your order using the tracking number on our website or the carrier's website.

## Lost or Damaged Packages
- If your package appears lost, please wait 48 hours after the estimated delivery date before contacting support.
- For damaged packages, please contact support within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage.
- We will reship or refund orders for packages confirmed lost by the carrier.
