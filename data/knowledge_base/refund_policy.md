# Refund Policy - VoiceOps Store

## Refund Eligibility
- Refunds are available for items returned within the 30-day return window.
- Refunds are processed only after the returned item has been received and inspected.
- Items must meet all return eligibility criteria to qualify for a refund.

## Refund Methods
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the purchase.
- If the original payment method is no longer available, a store credit will be issued.
- Store credits do not expire and can be used on any future purchase.

## Refund Timeline
- Once we receive your return, inspection takes 1-2 business days.
- After approval, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.
- Credit card refunds may take an additional 3-5 business days to appear on your statement.
- Store credit refunds are applied immediately after approval.

## Partial Refunds
- Partial refunds may be issued for items returned in less-than-original condition.
- A restocking fee of up to 15% may be applied for items showing signs of use.
- Partial refunds will be communicated before processing.

## Refund for Cancelled Orders
- Orders cancelled before shipment receive a full refund within 1-2 business days.
- Orders cancelled after shipment must follow the standard return process.
- Shipping charges for the original order are non-refundable unless the cancellation is due to our error.

## Non-Refundable Items
- Final sale items are not eligible for refunds.
- Gift cards are non-refundable.
- Shipping charges are non-refundable except in cases of our error.
- Items returned after the 30-day window are not eligible for refunds.

## Disputes
- If you disagree with a refund decision, please contact customer support within 14 days.
- We will review your case and provide a final determination within 5 business days.
- All refund disputes are handled by our customer support team.
