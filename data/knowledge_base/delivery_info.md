# Delivery Information - VoiceOps Store

## Delivery Methods
We offer three delivery options:
- **Standard Delivery**: 5-7 business days, free on orders over $50
- **Express Delivery**: 2-3 business days, $12.99
- **Overnight Delivery**: Next business day, $24.99

## Delivery Areas
- We deliver to all 50 US states and Washington D.C.
- We deliver to select international destinations.
- We do not deliver to PO Boxes for express or overnight shipments.
- Remote areas may experience additional delivery time.

## Delivery Tracking
- A tracking number is emailed to you once your order ships.
- You can track your delivery on our website under "My Orders."
- Tracking updates are provided by the carrier and may have a delay of up to 24 hours.
- Delivery confirmation is provided upon successful delivery.

## Delivery Issues

### Package Not Received
- Check your tracking status for the latest update.
- If tracking shows "Delivered" but you haven't received your package:
  - Check with neighbors or building management.
  - Check other entrances or hidden delivery areas.
  - Wait 24 hours as carriers sometimes mark packages as delivered prematurely.
  - Contact our support team with your order number.

### Wrong Address
- If you provided an incorrect address, contact support immediately.
- Address corrections are possible only before the package is shipped.
- If the package has shipped to the wrong address, we will work with the carrier to redirect it.
- If redirection fails, a replacement will be shipped at no additional cost (one-time courtesy).

### Delayed Delivery
- Delays can occur due to weather, holidays, or carrier issues.
- We will proactively notify you if your delivery is significantly delayed.
- If your delivery is more than 5 business days past the estimated date, contact support.
- Compensation or expedited reshipment may be offered for significant delays.

### Damaged During Delivery
- Inspect your package upon delivery.
- Report damage within 48 hours of delivery.
- Provide photos of the damaged packaging and product.
- We will arrange a replacement or refund.

## Signature Requirements
- Orders over $200 require a signature upon delivery.
- If you are not available to sign, the carrier will leave a delivery notice.
- You can authorize release without signature in your account settings for orders under $200.

## Delivery Hours
- Standard deliveries occur Monday through Saturday.
- Express and overnight deliveries are available Monday through Friday.
- No deliveries on federal holidays.
