# Escalation Policy - VoiceOps Store

## When to Escalate
Customer support interactions should be escalated to a human agent when:

1. **Customer explicitly requests a human agent.**
2. **The issue cannot be resolved by the automated system** after reasonable attempts.
3. **The customer expresses strong dissatisfaction** or frustration that the automated agent cannot adequately address.
4. **The issue involves sensitive information** such as fraud, identity theft, or legal matters.
5. **Repeated failed attempts** to resolve the issue (3 or more unsuccessful interactions on the same topic).
6. **Complex billing disputes** involving amounts over $100.
7. **Warranty claims** that require manual inspection or approval.
8. **Account security concerns** such as unauthorized access or suspicious activity.
9. **Multi-order issues** that span multiple orders or complex scenarios.
10. **The agent's confidence in resolving the issue is low.**

## Escalation Process
1. The automated agent summarizes the conversation and customer issue.
2. A support ticket is created with all relevant details.
3. The customer is informed that they are being transferred to a human agent.
4. The estimated wait time is communicated to the customer.
5. All conversation context is passed to the human agent.
6. The human agent reviews the context before connecting with the customer.

## Escalation Priorities
- **Urgent**: Fraud, security breaches, safety concerns → Immediate response
- **High**: Billing disputes over $100, repeated failures → Response within 1 hour
- **Medium**: Complex returns, warranty claims → Response within 4 hours
- **Low**: General inquiries beyond AI capability → Response within 24 hours

## Customer Communication During Escalation
- Always thank the customer for their patience.
- Clearly explain why the issue is being escalated.
- Provide a reference number for the escalation.
- Give an estimated response time.
- Offer to continue the conversation via the customer's preferred channel (phone, email, chat).

## Follow-up
- Human agents must follow up within the committed timeframe.
- If resolution requires more time, the customer must be updated proactively.
- Escalation outcomes are tracked for quality improvement.
- All escalated conversations are reviewed weekly for training purposes.
