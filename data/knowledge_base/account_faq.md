# Account FAQ - VoiceOps Store

## Creating an Account
- Visit our website and click "Sign Up" or "Create Account."
- You will need to provide your name, email address, and a password.
- Passwords must be at least 8 characters long and include a number.
- You will receive a verification email after registration.

## Managing Your Account
- You can update your name, email, phone number, and shipping addresses in your account settings.
- Password changes require your current password for security.
- You can view your order history, saved addresses, and payment methods in your account.

## Account Security
- Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) for additional security.
- Never share your account credentials with anyone.
- If you suspect unauthorized access, change your password immediately and contact support.
- We will never ask for your password via email or phone.

## Forgotten Password
- Click "Forgot Password" on the login page.
- Enter the email address associated with your account.
- You will receive a password reset link valid for 24 hours.
- If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

## Account Deletion
- You can request account deletion by contacting customer support.
- Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
- Your order history will be retained for legal and warranty purposes.
- Active orders must be completed or cancelled before account deletion.
- Allow up to 30 days for full data removal from all systems.

## Account Suspension
- Accounts may be suspended for violations of our terms of service.
- Suspicious activity such as multiple failed payment attempts may trigger a temporary suspension.
- Contact customer support if you believe your account was suspended in error.
- Suspended accounts cannot place orders or access account features.

## Communication Preferences
- You can manage your email notification preferences in account settings.
- You can opt out of marketing emails while still receiving order-related notifications.
- Order confirmation, shipping, and delivery emails cannot be disabled.
- SMS notifications can be enabled for delivery updates.
